First look
  • Black screen with power LED on: PLA (U17) first, then the 6510 and VIC-II.
  • Garbage on screen or dead keys: PLA or the RAMs; swap the 4164s in pairs.
  • No sound only: SID (U18) is socketed on most boards; try a known good one.
