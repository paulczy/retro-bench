Each data cell quotes all four requested fields. `<absent>` means no field exists. `verify` is the pin flag; `connector.verify` is separately shown because they are not interchangeable. Source-reading cells cover signal, electrical role, description and any expected voltage/frequency. Ordinary ground=0 V and nominal supply labels are circuit inferences, not measured values. No hardware measurements were made.

OK means the inspected sources support the row; WRONG means at least one requested field contradicts the drawing (a fair conflict note does not make a wrong electrical type correct); UNPROVABLE means at least one specific subclaim lacks drawing proof, or the drawings give incompatible readings. UNPROVABLE is not a claim that the entire pin entry is false. Text-only corroboration is explicitly identified. Bus rows were traced in order through numbered contacts; no missing evidence rows are defaulted to OK.

## Limits

No restricted lending book or third-party FAQ was used as proof.
