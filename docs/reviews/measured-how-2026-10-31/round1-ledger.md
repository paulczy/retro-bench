# Measured-condition fact-check ledger

Reviewed 2026-10-31. Complete scope: 117 conditions, 28 measured records, four glossary entries. Indices are **1-based within each conditions array**. Inventory was evaluated from the actual JavaScript, independently of the author log. Original text is preserved in [inventory.json](inventory.json); abbreviations below identify each condition.

Verdicts assess the whole condition; an unsupported added detail makes a partially supported row OVERSTATED. WRONG means a directly conflicting reading. UNPROVABLE means unreachable or silent. A source stating a condition is not independent proof that the underlying engineering claim is universally true.

Every cited document was downloaded/opened. Relevant PDF pages were rendered and visually inspected, including scanned pages; enlarged crops are in [pages/](pages/). Initial HTTP failures were recovered using browser User-Agent headers or the Internet Archive item storage host. No final source remained unreachable. [sources.json](sources.json) maps exact cited URLs to retained downloads; the source index below provides readable titles.

Generic/forbidden-source check: repeated meter, ground and x10 instructions are actually present in each cited machine manual; repetition alone is not evidence of imported generic advice. No measured condition was found to require Classic Repair Toolbox / Commodore Repair Toolbox as its source. This is a provenance-evidence finding, not proof of the author's browsing history. Unsupported generic glossary diagnostics are flagged below. The 1978 Apple II minimum-load claim is not independently established for II Plus.

IBM applicability was checked beyond the author's cited leaves: MAP PDF p.1 explicitly routes PC, XT and Portable PC to the same Power (PC) MAP. Their shared instructions are not rejected merely for the Power (PC) heading.
