## Findings and deletion decisions

1. **High — i760, model4 / m4-printer / pin17:** U94 is the wrong latch reference for the Model4. The Model4 manual says U66, and schematic8000173 sheet4 routes U66 Q7 (pin19) to printer17. Port F8 and data7 are correct. Change the reference to U66 or delete the inherited chip-specific clause. This can send a technician to the wrong IC. Principle: **subtract-before-you-add**.

2. **Medium — i699, ti994a / ti994a-io / pin41:** the detailed schematic makes IAQHA by ORing CPU IAQ with HOLDA; this is not raw instruction acquisition. Name it IAQHA (IAQ OR HOLDA). The simplified tables in the cited manual omit HOLDA; the detailed schematic and the servicing-manual HOLD/IAQ label resolve the conflict. Principle: **outcome-oriented-execution**.

3. **Medium — i189, ula_zx81 / pin36:** name A3' and the refresh address-driving role are supported. The cited circuit and replacement notes do not establish an input-sensing role for A3'; they do not prove the bidir value. **UNVERIFIABLE — source silent on the direction distinction.** Remove the bidir assertion or visibly mark direction unverified; retain the pin and supported function. Principle: **subtract-before-you-add**.

4. **Low — i695, ti994a / ti994a-power / pin2:** white-wire secondary tap is correct; the word centre is inaccurate. Sams specifies26.40VAC tapped at17.85VAC, rather than equal winding halves. Change to secondary tap/common white wire; delete centre. Principle: **subtract-before-you-add**.

Document grouping and reuse keep the check proportional: each base URL was downloaded as one document, and common pin tables were inspected for all related items together. No sub-agents were used. This applies **cost-aware-delegation** to the review method rather than inventing a code-review finding.
