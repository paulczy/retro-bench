## Findings

1. **HIGH — WRONG, additional pal40030 pin 20 (line 1031).** src/data/amstrad.js:352 changes VCC/power/+5 V to GND/ground/Ground. Maker PAL16L8 pin configuration (TI SRPS016, PDF1) says pin20 VCC, pin10 GND. The +4.75 to +5.25 V probe field even remains on the new ground row. Restore the original supply definition. A technician using this as a ground reference can short the +5 V rail. Principle: prove-it-works; fix-root-causes. [Maker PDF](fb-3/pal.pdf#page=1).

2. **MEDIUM — UNPROVEN, MSX1 first-ten step 2 (line 476).** src/data/msx.js:384 adds a specific 4116 pin-1/-5 V instruction while the step cites only the MSX cartridge standard. TDB pp.32-34 proves slot48 +12 V and slot50 -12 V, but no 4116 pinout. The power record source list contains no 4116 data sheet. Add the relevant manufacturer source before presenting that extra probe point as proven. Principle: prove-it-works.

3. **MEDIUM — UNPROVEN, CoCo1 first-ten step 5 (line 486).** src/data/tandy.js:1636 still states "Lose -5 V and the RAM fails." Sams PDF17 supports the regulator components; Bulletin CC:3 supports reversed C18 and capacitor overheating, not that categorical RAM outcome. Adding "in the supply sources" does not close the evidence gap. Either cite the RAM requirement/consequence or qualify/remove this statement. Principle: fix-root-causes; prove-it-works.

4. **LOW — UNPROVEN, ten retained MSX RCA convention rows (457-460, 462, 465-469).** src/data/msx.js:133 gives numbered centre/shell roles and a female connector from an application convention. The TDB p.29 says RCA 2-pin; it does not draw the contact wiring or specify gender. The cited p.14 gives only output level and connector family. Obtain a wiring/connector source or explicitly flag the inference. Principle: prove-it-works.

No finding is mapped to serialize-shared-state-mutations: this scope changes static data and yielded no evidence of a race or mutation-order defect.
