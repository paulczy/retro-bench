## Findings

1. HIGH -- additional:fb-1, src/data/ibm.js:172. Intel8088 Table1 p.3 says VCC40, GND1/20. New GND/ground at40 is false and dangerous; restore VCC/power/+5V. Retained probe already says+5V. Grounding the alleged ground pin shorts the supply.

2. HIGH -- 14:275, src/data/amstrad.js:83. CPC464 STROBE uses IC110 inputs9/10 ->output8 (service pp.15-16, PDF13). Shared fix incorrectly uses664/6128 inputs12/13 ->11. Keep corrected latch-Q7 origin but supply model-specific gate references.

3. HIGH -- 07:220, src/data/acorn.js:127. Master PB4/fire0 is IC8 pin14, not IC3 pin14. IC3 is the FDC. Master0143,000/C sheet2 proves it. ModelB IC3 reference was copied into Master. verify=true cannot cure a known false destination.

4. MEDIUM -- 08:163, src/data/amstrad.js:378. OE22 is pulled LOW through R1122K2 and connects to external ROMDIS; it is not hard tiedLOW. A HIGH ROMDIS disables ROM independently ofCS. All three CPU schematics show it. Original both-enables-low statement was correct.

5. MEDIUM -- 08:53/54, src/data/heathkit.js:1475/1549. Equal450ns minimum HIGH/LOW widths do not prove exact50% E-clock duty. Motorola p.4 specifies bounds with unequal maxima. Remove exact duty/claimed derivation or cite an actual specification.

6. MEDIUM -- 08:97,08:179,09:46. Unflagged firstTen5V steps retain centre-positive mapping, while14:210/211 flag the same physical mapping as unverified. Opened flowcharts/drawings establish5V but not5V barrel contact polarity. Obtain a cited contact drawing or propagate uncertainty to the power instructions.

7. MEDIUM -- 07:199. Master RGB6 nominal+5V is supported, but retained A few mA only is an uncited current limit. Master p.51 and schematicSK9 do not give that limit. Remove or source it.

8. LOW -- 15:2 through15:26. Log claims added verify flags, but pins/parent already had verify=true inHEAD. No new flag change occurred. Disclosure remains honest; restricted source prevents certification. Also07:399 still links p.31 while its supply procedure is p.32.
