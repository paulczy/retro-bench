# fa-apple adversarial fix review

Scope: all 60 data rows at docs/verification/factcheck-full-fixes-a.md:122-186, plus the one row under Additional corrections (fa-apple): 61 decisions. Review date: 2026-10-30.

CORRECT on a removal/verify row means the requested removal/flag was honestly implemented; it does not certify the retained flagged claims. WRONG means contradicted by the opened source. UNPROVEN means the precise remaining claim is not established by the cited documents. No product data was changed or committed.

Method: inspected git diff and current records; independently downloaded cited manuals, schematics and maker sheets into fa-apple/. Ran pdftotext -layout on each PDF. Scanned pages were rendered with pdftoppm -f N -l N -r 110 -png and visually inspected. Archive leaf n corresponds to PDF page n+1. Both printed and PDF page references appear below. curl -L -A Mozilla/5.0 was attempted; Windows Schannel returned SEC_E_NO_CREDENTIALS. Python urllib with the same user agent retrieved the same documents as fallback. IA OCR text is retained for search; schematic/waveform decisions use the rendered pages, not OCR alone.

## Findings

1. **HIGH — WRONG: 6502 pin 8 was changed from its actual VCC supply to GND.** src/data/apple.js:1269 now labels the +5 V pin as ground, yet retains probe:+5 V and the vcc term. MOS 6500 sheet printed/PDF p.10 explicitly labels 6502 pin8 VCC; ground is pins1 and21. A ground clip on8 shorts the logic supply. Restore Vcc/power/+5 V. The original value was correct. Principle: prove-it-works; fix-root-causes.
2. **MEDIUM — UNPROVEN: IIc MD7 three-state implementations were copied from IIe functional diagrams.** Lines1695 and1741 have no verify flag. IIc TRM pp.242-244 proves flag reporting/output function, but not the three-state electrical implementation; Sather Figs5.13a/7.1 concern IIe chips. Provide IIc electrical evidence or flag/remove the three-state qualifier. Principle: prove-it-works.
3. **MEDIUM — UNPROVEN: the new IIe supply diagnosis adds instructions beyond its cited source.** Line2321: p.160 proves short/disconnected cable/out-of-range protection and half-second restart. It does not give the new overload/loose-cable/good-load/regulation diagnostic sequence. Cite its procedure or flag that inference. Principle: prove-it-works; fix-root-causes.
4. **MEDIUM — UNPROVEN: retained DuoDisk rail routing is justified with two disconnected endpoint pin tables.** Lines1091-1092: TA37054 gives DB connector voltages; II slot tables give slot voltages. Neither establishes the DuoDisk controller wiring between them. Obtain its schematic or flag the routing notes. Principle: prove-it-works.
5. **LOW — documented II/II+ voltage acceptance ranges were discarded unnecessarily.** Lines58-61 and274: the old ranges all match the already-cited Red Book printed p.111 (PDF114): +5.0 +/-0.15, +11.8 +/-0.5, -5.2 +/-0.5, -12.0 +/-1 V. The new nominals are correct, but restoring ranges with that citation preserves useful diagnostic information. Principle: fix-root-causes (repair attribution rather than delete supported information).

No shared-state mutation or ordering finding: this scope is static reference data; serialize-shared-state-mutations does not apply to the observed defects.
