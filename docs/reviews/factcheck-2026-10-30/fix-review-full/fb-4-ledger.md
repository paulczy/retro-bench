## Findings

1. **HIGH H1: introduced power-pin reversal** - acorn.js:576, additional fb-4.65SC12 pin8 is VDD,+5V,not GND. Master0143,000/C sheet1 supply table explicitly lists IC14+5V8 andground1/5/21. Revert the new ground label. Principles:prove-it-works,fix-root-causes.
2. **HIGH H2:57 Electron ULA rows remain physically misdrawn** - acorn.js:946-947 keeps PLCC-68; app.js:622-627 uses top-centre pin1. Electron Service Manual8/PDF14 specifies quad-in-line,pin1 top-left,anticlockwise. Summary-only change cannot repair geometry. This is unresolved existing behavior, not a newly introduced package edit. Principles:prove-it-works,fix-root-causes.
3. **MEDIUM M1:Dragon E/Q scope rate lacks SAM state** - dragon.js:30-34,rows28:199/200/202/203. SAM5-333/PDF11 documents /16 slow and /8 fast plus address-dependent mode. Add reset-default slow precondition and other valid rates; otherwise a valid fast clock looks wrong. Principles:prove-it-works,fix-root-causes.
4. **MEDIUM M2:generic reset probe exceeds source** - ibm.js:198,row22:203. Intel /RES data cannot prove Stays LOW with a bad PSU. RC reset can release while another rail fails. Remove or qualify using a cited board/power-good circuit. Principle:prove-it-works.
5. **MEDIUM M3:removed XT use conflicts with cited text** - ibm.js:830,row25:178. XT3-3/PDF68 explicitly assigns-5V to diskette PLL. Do not silently delete and call this source-proven; reconcile text with the diskette schematic,record a conflict,or narrow to nominal supply. Principles:prove-it-works,fix-root-causes.

No serialize-shared-state-mutations finding in this data-only review. Shared builders and machine-specific connector variants were inspected.
