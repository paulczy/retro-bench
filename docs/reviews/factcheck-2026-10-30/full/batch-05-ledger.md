## Link/applicability issues

- S07 and S08 are inaccessible Wayback citations, not proven deleted. Replace with the accessible original Carlsen articles or preserve an audited local copy.
- S15, S16 and S14 are connector-name tables; they do not support added electrical levels, rates or procedures. See the corresponding UNVERIFIABLE rows.
- S28 is a Plus/4 map: its video pin 8 is NC, unlike C16 sheet 251788 (+5 V). It cannot prove C16-specific power wiring or Plus/4-specific C16 component designators.
- S18 and S19 are C16 manuals. Reusing their U11/Q5/D12 routing as proof for Plus/4 is unsupported.
- S17 PDF 10 is drawing sheet 5 (CRTC); it does support pin 23 E, but not the frequency by itself.
- S09 and S11 are different sheets of drawing 320349. Rows 102/109 use both downloaded sheets for the complete clock chain; their individual citation URLs should be expanded to show both sheets.
- S18 PDF 11 and 15 are pinout pages; S19 PDF 11/13 combines them with schematic sheets. The zimmers PDF rendered those requested pages successfully in this run.

No hardware was energized or probed; this review verifies documentary claims only.
