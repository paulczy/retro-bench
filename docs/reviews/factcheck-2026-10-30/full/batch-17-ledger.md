# Batch-17 fact-check ledger

Scope: 234 supplied items, data snapshot commit 4b85bd0. Every item has one decision. No application data changed.

Decision rule: WRONG takes precedence if any material claim contradicts a source. UNVERIFIABLE means the cited source is silent on at least one stated claim; it does not assert that the underlying claim is false. CONFIRMED covers the complete record, including pin references and numeric values. Scope-photo values are reported captures, not guaranteed component limits. Evidence names PDF pages (1-based), with printed pages where useful.

Acquisition: all 18 Archive.org manuals plus the cited Bitsavers PDF and Atarimania FAQ were retrieved once into batch-17/. Initial curl -L -A Mozilla/5.0 failed with SEC_E_NO_CREDENTIALS; Python urllib with the same User-Agent retrieved the actual documents. PDFs were extracted with pdftotext -layout; cited scanned pages were rendered with pdftoppm at 110 dpi and inspected. The 1581 foldout required a resized view and a separate switch crop. No documents were dead or blocked.
