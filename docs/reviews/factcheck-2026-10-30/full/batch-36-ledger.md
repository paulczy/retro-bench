## Source access and source defects

The required curl -L -A Mozilla/5.0 downloads were attempted first. Windows curl failed locally with SEC_E_NO_CREDENTIALS; Git curl did not support alternate TLS backend. Python urllib fetched the actual documents as a fallback; TLS verification was bypassed only for the public Regensburg scan mirror whose certificate chain was untrusted. All source reads were of fetched content, not record wording.

Archive URL IDs 0,2,6,8,11,12,13 returned HTTP 429; IDs 14,15 refused the connection. These are blocked/unavailable in this session, not demonstrated permanently dead. The five live Defence Force wiki endpoints returned HTTP 403. Live CPCWiki returned HTTP403.

Issue-7 schematic IDs 0 and 6 recovered as original scans at https://homepages.uni-regensburg.de/~hep09515/oric1/oric1-1s.gif and https://homepages.uni-regensburg.de/~hep09515/oric1/oric1-2s.gif . Defence Force ULA/expansion/keyboard wiki archive pages remained blocked. Their relevant connections were checked in the independently cited Oric Owner schematics, actual issue-7 scan mirror, manuals and Mike Brown guides.

Atmos manual Appendix 11 printed p.285 (PDF290) labels RGB1 RED / RGB3 BLUE; both cited circuit revisions instead label RGB1 BLUE / RGB3 RED. Items78 and80 follow schematic circuit evidence and are not marked WRONG. The citation should warn about this source conflict.

No claim was marked CONFIRMED from an unavailable archive page. No physical motherboard was tested; findings establish documentary correspondence, not live electrical measurements.
