# batch-04 fact-check ledger

Input: commit 4b85bd0 read-only review data; 338 items, indices 0-337. No application data was changed.

Decision policy: CONFIRMED means the substantive claim is supported by the cited document(s), including relevant sections elsewhere in the same document. If a pin label is supported but an added numerical, diagnostic, wiring, or machine-state assertion is not, the entire item is UNVERIFIABLE and the evidence identifies the precise unsupported clause. WRONG takes precedence when an explicit contradiction is established. Source conflicts are recorded rather than silently resolved from memory. An already-downloaded primary service schematic can disprove a hazardous claim even if the offending record omitted that schematic citation; those cases are identified explicitly. This is a document audit, not a live-board measurement.

Sources were downloaded once per URL using curl -L -A Mozilla/5.0 into batch-04/, extracted with pdftotext -layout (UTF-8), and relevant scanned drawings were rendered with pdftoppm at 110 dpi. Initial curl status and recovery URLs are in download-status.json and alternate-status.json; corrected HTTPS diagnostics are in https-recovery.json and body-recovery.json. Page numbers below are printed pages unless marked PDF or sheet. C128 alternate scan has shifting PDF offsets; its actual printed page labels were inspected.

Principles: every unsupported or contradictory assertion maps to prove-it-works. Incorrect signal names, timing edges, reset semantics, and copied C64-mode assumptions also map to fix-root-causes. serialize-shared-state-mutations has no substantive application to this static data-only audit; there were no shared-state mutations.
