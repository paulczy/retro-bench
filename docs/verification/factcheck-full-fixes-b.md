## Disagreements with the reviewer

1. **Electron ULA package (b27 i69-125, 57 rows).** The manual (SM p. 8) does say "68-pin quad in-line package", pins
   anticlockwise from the top left. `pkg` stays `'PLCC-68'` because that string is the app's key for the square
   68-contact drawing; any other value draws a 34 + 34 DIP, which is wrong for this square carrier. The summary now carries
   the manual's wording.
2. **IBM MDA drive pins (b17 i121-122, WRONG).** Sams CSCS2-A p. 3 (scan n86) labels HORIZ DRIVE J3-8 and VERT DRIVE J3-9,
   as the data had. Kept; the scope notes now quote the Sams labels.
3. **IBM bus clock probe (b17 i117).** Sams p. 59 shows CLK88 entering U15 (74LS244) on pin 13 and the output on pin 7
   driving R20 to B20, where the 6 V photo is taken. Kept; the note now says so.
4. **CPC664 +12 V (b09 i44, WRONG).** On the 664 cassette control drawing (SM PDF p. 29) the LA4140 (IC301) is fed from
   the +5 V monitor line through R322 (4.7 ohm), not from +12 V. The rail keeps "drive supply" and states this; the range
   was removed.
5. **Dragon 64 cassette out (b30 i181, WRONG).** R40 33K agrees. The 68K label by PL4 is illegible on CD41505 sheet 1, and
   R14 on the same sheet is the 4K7 pull-up by IC4, so R14 is not taken. Pin desc says "a 68 kOhm series resistor"; a
   `conflict` records both readings.
6. **MSX RCA centre/shell pins (b16 i62-77, 10 rows).** Pin 1 = centre and pin 2 = shell is the app's `rca` convention for
   every phono jack, not a claim about the MSX book. Unchanged.
7. **Page ranges that follow from a definition (b07 i310, 312, 339):** FRED is page &FC (&FC00-&FCFF), JIM page &FD, the
   Tube &FEE0-&FEFF. Kept; the BBC Advanced User Guide was added to the Master connector sources.
8. **Chip pin numbers in `where` (several MSX, Amstrad, IBM and Acorn rows).** "Z80A pin 11", "65SC12 pin 9 to pin 25" are
   read from the chip's own pin table in the app. For MSX, Amstrad and IBM the chip pin pages were added to the power
   sources; the Master firstTen step 6 (b07 i403) is unchanged.
9. **Already compliant:** HERO Jr RS-232 is already `gender: 'female'` (b19 i145-146); every HERO 1 board-connector pin
   that cites the restricted HERO book (b15, 201 rows) already had `verify: true`; BBC B auxiliary power is a
   `verify: true` connector whose summary says Figure 10 has no numbers (b08 i10-14).
