# C64 mainboard photos for Retro Bench

Research date: 2026-11-02. Scope: photos of the C64 boards defined in `src/data/schematics.js`. Nothing was added to the app and nothing was committed.

Preview thumbnails (600 px wide or less) are in `P:\retro-bench\.tmp\c64-photos\thumbs\`. Full-size originals used for label checks are in `.tmp\c64-photos\raw2\` and `raw3\`. Both folders are inside `.tmp` only.

## How photos were checked

- Every candidate was viewed as a preview. Where a label was needed, a crop of the full-size original was viewed.
- "Pixel size" is the original file size from the source.
- "Angle" describes the shot as seen in the preview: square-on, mild tilt, or steep oblique.
- Board identity comes from text printed on the board (quoted) or from the source's own statement. Where neither was legible, the photo is marked as unconfirmed.

Verdicts: GOOD = clean whole-board shot, 2000 px or more, identity confirmed, license clear. USABLE = usable with a stated issue (size, license not confirmed, or identity inferred). POOR = steep angle, in-case crop, or provenance problem.

## Licence notes that apply to several candidates

- **Commons CC BY / CC BY-SA** files: these are on the app's allowed list. CC BY-SA requires the adapted work (photo with overlays) to be shared under the same or a compatible licence. The owner should confirm that the app's own licence is compatible.
- **c64-wiki** states "Content is available under GFDL unless otherwise noted" (site footer). I could not see a per-file licence on the file pages I fetched. The GFDL is on the app's allowed list, but each c64-wiki file needs its file page checked in a browser before use. Several c64-wiki file pages say "This file is from a shared repository", which means the original description lives elsewhere and the uploader may not be the photographer.
- **Breadbox64** (breadbox64.com) pages carry "© breadbox64.com 2026". Treat as all rights reserved. Not usable without written permission.

## Assy 326298 (original)

### C64motherboard.jpg (Wikimedia Commons)
- File page: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:C64motherboard.jpg
- Image: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/86/C64motherboard.jpg
- Pixel size: 2048 x 953 (original). Preview: `thumbs\C64motherboard.jpg`.
- Angle and coverage: top-down, square-on. Whole board is in frame, including both cartridge edge connectors, the power and video connectors and the RF modulator. No case visible.
- Visible text: source description "Commodore 64 Rev A. PAL motherboard (1982)". The ROM markings read "901227-01" and "MOS 901227-02 1982". The assy number is not legible. The right edge is cut through "ASSY", "REV A" and "COMMO...".
- Identity: the photo is confirmed as a 1982 Rev A PAL board. That it is 326298 is inferred from the date, the 4164 RAMs and the 2364-style ROMs, not from a printed number.
- Licence: CC BY 2.0.
- Author and attribution: Bill Bertram (Commons author field "Own work"). Attribution: "Photo: Bill Bertram, CC BY 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons" with the file-page link.
- Permits app use: yes. Attribution only, no share-alike, no non-commercial limit.
- Issues: the uploader on Commons is Pixel8, not the author. Confirm that Bill Bertram took the photo. The assy number is not visible.
- Verdict: USABLE (identity inferred, assy not visible).

### Other 326298 leads (not usable as found)
